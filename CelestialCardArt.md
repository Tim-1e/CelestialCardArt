# 天体卡面 · v1.1.1

以天体奇观和储君为主题的卡牌与遗物美术包。只修改美术显示，不改变卡牌数值或战斗规则。

## 收录内容

18张卡牌、20套动态画稿，宇宙冷漠和地形改造各有A/B两套；7件遗物美术、13套人物素材。普通卡面与先古全幅均支持动态，共用原分辨率12 fps压缩视频，按各自窗口裁切。

## 默认设置与更换方法

全新配置默认18张卡使用本包主稿先古全幅，动画总开关和全部播放范围开启。「仅升级后播放」默认开启；贪婪不可升级，因此无需升级也播放，但仍遵守总开关与播放范围。已有偏好（包括Skin Changer选择）继续保留。

- 进入「模组设置 → 天体卡面 → 卡面选择与预览」，先选卡牌，再选普通／先古及A/B套，切换即时保存。
- 右侧同步显示所选版式的静态与动态效果；游戏原版及其他来源也可预览。
- 安装Skin Changer后，下拉框列出该卡全部可用来源，可直接选择。「跟随SC分类」按SC的分类优先级决定。
- 点击「在动态浏览台放大查看」，可欣赏实际卡框及无遮挡大图，切换图稿和版式，暂停、慢放或拖动时间。浏览不改变对局播放设置。
- 本页「动态卡面」可调整总开关、播放范围和升级条件；素材库与原取景台保持静态。
- 首次进入主菜单有一次性使用提示，之后启动和更新不重复显示。

推荐搭配[Skin Changer](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3787302680)，在「模组设置 → Skin Changer」按卡牌或分类管理原版及其他来源。两边共用单卡选择；SC为可选项，未安装时也能使用本包设置。

## 安装与兼容

需要[RitsuLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295) 0.5.20或以上。验证环境为Windows、游戏v0.111.0。更新后重启游戏；若已有手动安装版，请移出旧副本，避免重复加载。
同一图稿共用播放器，隐藏时暂停，退出后释放。已检查18卡、40种图稿/版式组合、原版与SC来源预览、升级规则及播放器释放；完整对局及所有模组组合并未穷尽测试。反馈请附游戏版本、模组及复现步骤。

# English — Celestial Card Art v1.1.1

## Language note

Sorry, an English version of the mod is not available yet. Its interface and descriptions are not localized into English. We will add localization if there is demand; please let us know in the comments.

## Contents and defaults

A cosmic-themed card and relic art pack for the Regent: 18 cards, 20 animated artworks, 7 relic artworks and a gallery of 13 character artworks. Cosmic Indifference and Terraforming have Set A/B choices. Both normal portraits and Ancient full art animate using the same original-resolution 12 fps compressed video, cropped to their respective windows. Artwork only; no changes to card stats or combat rules.
Fresh settings use the main Ancient artwork for all 18 cards, with all playback locations enabled. Play only after upgrading is ON by default, except Greed, which cannot be upgraded. Greed still respects the master switch and playback-location settings. Existing preferences, including Skin Changer choices, are preserved.

## Choosing and previewing artwork

- Open Mod Settings → 天体卡面 → 卡面选择与预览. Choose a card, then its normal/Ancient layout and Set A/B artwork. Changes save immediately.
- Two previews show static and animated artwork in the selected layout. Original game art and other providers also display normally.
- With Skin Changer installed, the dropdown lists all sources available for that card. Following the SC category uses its category priority order.
- Choose 在动态浏览台放大查看 to view the actual card and a larger unobstructed image. Switch sets/layouts, pause, slow playback or scrub the timeline. Browsing does not change gameplay preferences.
- Use 动态卡面 on the same page for playback switches and the upgrade-only option. The gallery and cropping preview remain static.
- A one-time main-menu toast explains the defaults and settings path. It does not repeat on later launches or updates.

We recommend [Skin Changer](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3787302680) for managing artwork by card or category under Mod Settings → Skin Changer. Both interfaces share per-card choices. SC is optional; this pack also works without it.

## Installation and feedback

Requires [RitsuLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295) 0.5.20 or later. Tested on Windows with game v0.111.0. Restart after updates and remove any old manual copy to avoid duplicate loading.
Cards with the same artwork share a video player; hidden playback pauses and unused players are released. Checks cover the 18 cards, 40 artwork/layout combinations, original and SC previews, upgrade rules and cleanup. Full runs and all mod combinations are not exhaustively tested. Reports should include game version, mod list and reproduction steps.
